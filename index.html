Our Story

A Haven in the Heart of the Forest

Nestled among ancient trees and overlooking the serene waters of the Staudamm, Waldbaude am Staudamm has been a beloved destination for nature enthusiasts, families, and food lovers since its founding. Our restaurant embodies the perfect harmony between traditional German hospitality and the untouched beauty of the Thuringian Forest.

Every dish we serve tells a story of local heritage, crafted with ingredients sourced from nearby farms and prepared according to time-honored recipes passed down through generations. Our beer garden, shaded by century-old oaks, offers an unparalleled atmosphere where the gentle rustling of leaves accompanies every sip of fresh German beer.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before visiting Waldbaude am Staudamm.

Do you accept reservations? ▼

Yes, we highly recommend making a reservation, especially on weekends and holidays. You can reserve by phone at +49 177 2699755 or use our online reservation form. Walk-ins are also welcome based on availability.

Are dogs allowed? ▼

Absolutely! We are a dog-friendly establishment. Well-behaved dogs are welcome in our outdoor beer garden area. We even provide fresh water bowls for your four-legged friends upon request.

Do you have outdoor seating? ▼

Yes, our beautiful beer garden is the highlight of our restaurant. With seating for over 100 guests under century-old oak trees, it is the perfect place to enjoy your meal surrounded by nature.

Is parking available? ▼

Yes, we have a free parking lot directly adjacent to the restaurant with ample space for cars and bicycles. Additional parking is available along Hammerweg for busy days.

Is the restaurant family friendly? ▼

Very much so! We welcome families with children of all ages. We have a dedicated play area in the beer garden, high chairs available, and a kids menu with smaller portions at reduced prices.

Do you offer vegetarian meals? ▼

Yes, we offer several vegetarian options including our homemade soups, fresh salads, cheese spaetzle, and vegetarian strudel. Please ask your server for the current vegetarian specials.

What is your beer selection? ▼

We serve a variety of authentic German beers including Pilsner, Weissbier, Dunkel, and seasonal specialties. We also offer Radler, alcohol-free beer, and craft beer selections. All beers are served fresh on tap.

Are your cakes homemade? ▼

Yes! Our cakes and pastries are baked fresh every morning in our kitchen using traditional recipes and high-quality ingredients. The Black Forest cake and apple strudel are particularly popular among our guests.

Do you accept credit cards? ▼

Yes, we accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) as well as debit cards and cash. Contactless payment is also available.

Is the restaurant wheelchair accessible? ▼

Yes, our main restaurant area and restrooms are wheelchair accessible. The beer garden has some gravel paths, but we have designated accessible seating areas near the entrance. Please let us know if you need assistance.

Can I host a private event? ▼

Yes, we offer private event hosting for birthdays, corporate gatherings, weddings, and other celebrations. We can accommodate groups up to 80 people. Contact us for customized menus and pricing.

Do you have Wi-Fi? ▼

Yes, complimentary Wi-Fi is available throughout the restaurant and beer garden. Ask your server for the password. However, we encourage you to disconnect and enjoy the natural surroundings!

Are you open on holidays? ▼

We are open most holidays with regular hours, but we may have limited hours on Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, and New Year's Eve. Please check our Facebook page or call ahead for holiday hours.

Do you offer takeout? ▼

Yes, we offer takeout for most menu items including our homemade cakes. Please call ahead to place your order, and we will have it ready for pickup. We do not offer delivery service.

What are your opening hours? ▼

We are open daily from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Our kitchen serves food until 5:30 PM. During peak summer months, we may extend hours on weekends. Follow us on Facebook for updates.

Is there a dress code? ▼

There is no formal dress code. We welcome guests in casual hiking gear, cycling outfits, or smart casual attire. Come as you are and enjoy the relaxed, natural atmosphere of our beer garden.

Do you have allergen information? ▼

Yes, we provide detailed allergen information for all menu items. Please inform your server about any allergies or dietary restrictions, and our kitchen team will accommodate your needs safely.

Can I buy gift vouchers? ▼

Yes, gift vouchers are available in any denomination and make a wonderful present for friends and family. They can be purchased at the restaurant or by phone.

Is there a playground for children? ▼

Yes, we have a small play area in the beer garden with swings and a sandbox. Children can play safely while parents enjoy their meal within sight.

Do you serve breakfast? ▼

We serve a selection of breakfast items from 10:00 AM including fresh bread rolls, pastries, coffee, and tea. Our full breakfast menu is available on weekends until 12:00 PM.
